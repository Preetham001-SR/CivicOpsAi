# Policy Document: Emergency Response Protocols for Infrastructure

## POL-2024-001 - Infrastructure Emergency Response Protocol

### 1. Purpose
Establish standardized emergency response procedures for critical infrastructure failures that pose immediate risk to public safety.

### 2. Scope
Applies to all Public Works, Transportation, and Utilities departments responding to:
- Sinkholes and major pavement failures
- Bridge structural concerns
- Major flooding events
- Traffic signal system failures
- Downed power lines affecting traffic control

### 3. Activation Criteria
**Level 1 - Immediate (0-2 hours)**:
- Sinkhole > 1m diameter or depth
- Bridge closure required
- Flooding > 30cm on traveled way
- Traffic signals dark at major intersection (> 20,000 ADT)
- Any condition requiring immediate road closure

**Level 2 - Urgent (2-24 hours)**:
- Pavement failure affecting lane
- Partial flooding of roadway
- Traffic signal malfunction (flashing/conflicting)
- Sign knockdown at controlled intersection

**Level 3 - Expedited (24-72 hours)**:
- Significant pothole on arterial
- Streetlight outage at intersection
- Drainage backup threatening property

### 4. Response Team Structure
**Incident Commander**: Senior PW Superintendent
**Safety Officer**: PW Safety Coordinator
**Operations**: Field crews, traffic control
**Logistics**: Materials, equipment, vendors
**Planning**: Engineering, detour routes
**Public Info**: Communications, 311 coordination

### 5. Communication Protocol
- 311: Citizen reports → automated dispatch
- PW Dispatch: Crew assignment, tracking
- EOC Notification: Level 1 incidents only
- Media: PIO handles (Level 1-2)
- Elected Officials: CMO briefs (Level 1 only)

### 6. Documentation Requirements
- Incident report within 2 hours (Level 1)
- Daily situation reports (Level 1-2)
- After-action report within 14 days
- Cost tracking from onset
- Photo/video documentation mandatory

### 7. Mutual Aid Agreements
- Adjacent cities: Equipment sharing
- County: Vacuum trucks, heavy equipment
- Caltrans: State highway support
- Private contractors: Pre-qualified list

### 8. Training and Exercises
- Tabletop exercise: Quarterly
- Full-scale exercise: Annually
- New employee orientation: Within 30 days
- After-action reviews: Within 30 days

## POL-2024-002 - Citizen Complaint Prioritization Matrix

### 1. Priority Factors Matrix

| Factor | Weight | Critical (4) | High (3) | Medium (2) | Low (1) |
|--------|--------|--------------|----------|------------|---------|
| **Safety Risk** | 30% | Imminent hazard | Probable injury | Possible injury | Nuisance |
| **Traffic Impact** | 20% | Arterial closure | Lane closure arterial | Collector lane | Local street |
| **Vulnerable Population** | 15% | School/hospital/senior | Transit corridor | High ped area | Standard |
| **Recurring Issue** | 15% | 3+ reports/30 days | 2 reports/30 days | 1 report/30 days | First report |
| **Regulatory/Legal** | 10% | ADA violation, consent decree | MUTCD non-compliance | Code violation | None |
| **Cost of Delay** | 10% | >$10k/day escalating | $5-10k/day | $1-5k/day | <$1k/day |

### 2. Priority Calculation
Priority Score = Sum of (Factor Weight × Factor Rating)
- **Critical**: Score ≥ 3.5
- **High**: Score 2.5 - 3.4
- **Medium**: Score 1.5 - 2.4
- **Low**: Score < 1.5

### 3. Response Time Standards
| Priority | Initial Response | Temporary Fix | Permanent Repair |
|----------|------------------|---------------|------------------|
| Critical | 2 hours | 24 hours | 7 days |
| High | 24 hours | 72 hours | 14 days |
| Medium | 72 hours | 7 days | 30 days |
| Low | 14 days | 30 days | Next cycle |

### 4. Escalation Triggers
- Missed response time → Auto-escalate to next priority
- Citizen complaint re-opened → Supervisor review
- Media inquiry → PIO + Dept Director notification
- Elected official inquiry → CMO + Dept Director response within 4 hours

### 5. Quality Assurance
- Random audit: 10% of closed complaints monthly
- Customer satisfaction survey: 20% sample
- Metrics dashboard: Real-time for supervisors
- Annual calibration workshop for intake staff

## POL-2024-003 - Material and Contractor Standards

### 1. Approved Materials List
**Asphalt**:
- Hot Mix: Caltrans Type A, PG 64-10
- Cold Mix: QPR 2000, EZ Street
- Tack Coat: SS-1h, CSS-1h

**Concrete**:
- Sidewalk: 3,500 psi, 6 sack, 4-6% air
- Curb/Gutter: 3,000 psi
- Structural: 4,000 psi minimum

**Signs**:
- Sheeting: 3M Type XI (diamond grade) mandatory
- Substrate: 0.080" aluminum
- Posts: 2" square tube, breakaway

**Streetlights**:
- Fixture: LED, 3000K, 0-10V dimming
- Pole: Round tapered steel, anchor base
- Controller: NEMA TS2 Type 2, cellular modem

### 2. Pre-Qualified Contractors
**Category A - Emergency (24/7)**:
- ABC Paving Co. - Asphalt, concrete
- XYZ Underground - Drainage, utilities
- Lightning Electric - Signals, lighting

**Category B - Routine Maintenance**:
- City Maintenance Services - General
- Green Earth Landscaping - Vegetation
- Bay Area Striping - Markings, signs

**Category C - Specialty**:
- BridgeTech Engineers - Structures
- Historic Preservation Group - Landmarks
- GeoStabilize - Slopes, retaining walls

### 3. Procurement Thresholds
- <$5,000: Direct purchase (P-card)
- $5,000-$25,000: Three quotes
- $25,000-$100,000: Informal bid
- >$100,000: Formal bid (Council approval)

### 4. Warranty Requirements
- Asphalt patches: 1 year
- Concrete: 2 years
- Signals/Lighting: 5 years
- Contractor workmanship: 1 year minimum

## POL-2024-004 - Climate Resilience Adaptation

### 1. Sea Level Rise Considerations
- All new drainage: +1ft freeboard above 2100 projections
- Pump station capacity: 25% safety factor
- Shoreline assets: Adaptive management plans

### 2. Heat Island Mitigation
- Cool pavement pilot: 10 blocks/year
- Tree canopy target: 30% coverage
- Green infrastructure: Bioswales required on >1acre projects

### 3. Storm Intensity Adaptation
- 100-year storm design: NOAA Atlas 14 + 20%
- Culvert sizing: Climate-adjusted IDF curves
- Emergency overflow paths: Mapped and maintained

## POL-2024-005 - Data and Technology Standards

### 1. Asset Management
- All assets in CityWorks CMMS
- GIS integration mandatory
- Condition assessments: 3-year cycle
- Mobile field entry: Required for all crews

### 2. Sensor Networks
- Smart streetlights: Environmental sensors
- Flood gauges: 15 key locations
- Traffic sensors: All signalized intersections
- Data platform: Open data portal (monthly refresh)

### 3. AI/ML Applications
- Pothole prediction model: Retrained quarterly
- Pavement condition indexing: Annual
- Resource allocation optimization: Monthly
- Human-in-the-loop: All automated decisions reviewable

## POL-2024-006 - Interdepartmental Coordination

### 1. Standing Committees
- **Infrastructure Coordination**: Monthly (PW, Trans, Planning, Fire, Police)
- **Emergency Management**: Quarterly + as needed
- **Capital Planning**: Bi-annual (5-year CIP alignment)
- **Climate Resilience**: Quarterly

### 2. Shared Protocols
- **Dig Once**: Fiber/conduit with all pavement projects
- **Complete Streets**: All resurfacing includes bike/ped review
- **Utility Coordination**: 6-month lookahead mandatory
- **Special Events**: Unified permit process

### 3. Information Sharing
- Common GIS platform
- Shared CMMS access (read-only cross-dept)
- Joint public dashboard
- Unified 311 categorization
