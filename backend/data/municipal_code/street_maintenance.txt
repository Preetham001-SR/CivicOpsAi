# Municipal Code: Street Maintenance Standards
## Section 12.04.010 - Pothole Repair Standards

### 12.04.010.1 Definition and Classification
A pothole is defined as a bowl-shaped depression in the roadway surface where pavement material has been lost, creating a hazard to vehicular traffic. Potholes are classified by severity:

**Class A - Critical**: Diameter > 60cm OR depth > 10cm OR located on arterial road with > 20,000 ADT
- Response time: 24 hours
- Requires immediate temporary patch
- Permanent repair within 7 days

**Class B - High**: Diameter 30-60cm AND depth 5-10cm OR on collector road with > 5,000 ADT
- Response time: 72 hours
- Permanent repair within 14 days

**Class C - Standard**: Diameter < 30cm AND depth < 5cm on local/residential streets
- Response time: 14 days
- Scheduled with routine maintenance

### 12.04.010.2 Repair Materials and Methods
- Temporary patches: Cold mix asphalt (QPR or equivalent)
- Permanent repairs: Hot mix asphalt, minimum 50mm thickness
- Base repair required if subgrade failure detected
- Saw-cut edges required for permanent patches > 30cm

### 12.04.010.3 Inspection Requirements
- All potholes reported via 311 must be inspected within 48 hours
- Inspection report must include: dimensions, location (GPS), photos, traffic impact assessment
- Priority classification must be documented per 12.04.010.1

### 12.04.010.4 Documentation and Tracking
- All repairs logged in CMMS with work order number
- Before/after photos required
- Material quantities tracked
- Cost per repair tracked for budgeting

## Section 12.04.020 - Streetlight Maintenance

### 12.04.020.1 Outage Response Times
**Critical**: Intersections, school zones, mid-block crosswalks
- Response: 4 hours (24/7)
- Repair: 24 hours

**Standard**: Residential streets, alleys
- Response: 5 business days
- Repair: 14 business days

### 12.04.020.2 LED Conversion Program
- All replacements must use LED fixtures (3000K color temperature)
- Smart controllers required on arterial roads
- Photocell sensors mandatory

## Section 12.04.030 - Sidewalk Repair Standards

### 12.04.030.1 Trip Hazard Criteria
Vertical displacement > 12mm (1/2 inch) requires repair
- High priority: Near schools, hospitals, senior centers, transit stops
- Standard priority: Other locations

### 12.04.030.2 Repair Methods
- Grinding for displacements 12-25mm
- Panel replacement for displacements > 25mm
- Tree root mitigation required where applicable
- ADA compliance mandatory for all repairs

---
## Section 12.04.040 - Drainage and Stormwater

### 12.04.040.1 Catch Basin Maintenance
- Inspection: Annually before rainy season (April)
- Cleaning: When sediment > 60% of sump depth
- Emergency response: Flooding reports within 2 hours

### 12.04.040.2 Curb and Gutter Repair
- Deterioration affecting drainage flow: 30-day repair
- Structural failure: 72-hour temporary repair

---
## Section 12.04.050 - Traffic Sign Standards

### 12.04.050.1 Sign Replacement Criteria
- Retroreflectivity below MUTCD minimums
- Physical damage (bent, faded, graffiti)
- Obsolete or non-compliant legends

### 12.04.050.2 Priority Classification
**Immediate (24hr)**: Stop, Yield, Do Not Enter, Wrong Way, School Zone
**High (72hr)**: Speed limit, regulatory, warning signs on arterials
**Standard (14 days)**: Guide signs, parking signs, residential regulatory

---
## Section 12.04.060 - Jurisdictional Boundaries

### 12.04.060.1 City Responsibility
- All public streets within incorporated city limits
- Sidewalks adjacent to public property
- Streetlights on public streets

### 12.04.060.2 County/State Responsibility
- State highways (SR routes) - Caltrans
- County roads in unincorporated areas
- Freeway on/off ramps - Caltrans

### 12.04.060.3 Private Responsibility
- Private roads and driveways
- Sidewalks adjacent to private property (per 12.04.030)
- Streetlights on private property
